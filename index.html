One click to record the clip

YouTube doesn't let websites read its video directly, so LoopCut records the player while it plays your selection — just like a screen recorder, but only the video box.

  1. Click Start recording.
  2. Your browser asks to share — pick This tab and press Share.
  3. Keep this tab in view until it finishes (3.0s).

Works best in Chrome or Edge on desktop. Nothing is uploaded; the recording stops on its own.

Keyboard shortcuts

  1. Space Play / pause
  2. I Set start at playhead
  3. O Set end at playhead
  4. ← → Step one frame · with Shift: one second
  5. Enter Create GIF
